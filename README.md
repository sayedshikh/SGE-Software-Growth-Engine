# Software Growth Engine (SGE)

**A practical, guided workspace for discovering customer problems, evaluating opportunities, preparing respectful outreach, and tracking the commercial journey.**

> SGE is evolving. This public repository is a product and documentation hub—not a claim that every workflow is complete or independently validated.

## What SGE helps organize

- **Guided Discovery:** shape research around your business, product, audience, and goals.
- **Problem monitoring:** review signals from available public sources and feeds.
- **Evidence review:** inspect source links and distinguish observations from assumptions.
- **Opportunity intelligence:** evaluate problem/product fit using transparent heuristics.
- **Match & Reply Studio:** prepare context-aware drafts for human review.
- **Conversation workflow:** record stages and consent/decline boundaries.
- **Campaign journey:** connect opportunities, artifacts, leads and recorded outcomes.
- **Commercial tracking:** record costs and outcomes; calculate metrics only from entered data.
- **Next-step guidance:** deterministic recommendations based on recorded funnel state.

## Principles

- Free-first, practical workflows where feasible.
- Human review before external posting, messaging or consequential actions.
- No fabricated customer evidence, sales, metrics, or testimonials.
- No automatic posting or sending.
- Source provenance and uncertainty remain visible.
- No promise of sales, traffic, ranking, or AI-search placement.
