Practical guide · Zero-budget starting point

A free software growth stack for indie developers

Build discoverability, measure interest and improve your product marketing without starting with paid subscriptions. Free-plan limits and terms can change; verify them before relying on any service.

1. Measure search visibility and traffic

Google Search Console

Track organic search impressions, clicks, queries, indexing and technical search visibility. Use it to find pages that receive impressions but need better titles, intent alignment or content.

Google Analytics 4

Measure traffic acquisition and carefully selected events, such as visits to product profiles, outbound marketplace clicks and free-tool interactions. Avoid collecting unnecessary personal data and review consent/privacy requirements for your audience.

2. Publish and distribute without paid tools

  • GitHub Pages: host a static portfolio, guides and resources.
  • GitHub: document releases, issues, engineering decisions and demos where appropriate.
  • LinkedIn: publish problem-solving stories, product demonstrations and thoughtful technical posts.
  • YouTube: publish walkthroughs, short demos and explanations; keep claims tied to real product footage.
  • Relevant communities: contribute useful answers first; follow each community's promotional rules.
  • Facebook and other social platforms: repurpose content selectively, not as repetitive spam.

3. Create content from actual engineering work

Use one verified problem or product capability as the source for a guide, a concise engineering story, a demo and a social post. Do not invent customer stories, performance claims or test results.

4. Measure actions—not vanity metrics alone

  • Search impressions and clicks.
  • Visits to relevant product pages.
  • Clicks to verified marketplace listings.
  • Free-tool usage and completion (only if implemented and privacy-reviewed).
  • Newsletter subscriptions or inquiries, when consent and tracking are properly handled.
  • Actual purchases or revenue only when confirmed by seller/platform data.

5. Avoid spending until evidence supports it

Do not purchase SEO tools, ads, analytics upgrades or automation subscriptions simply to begin. Use free tiers and manual workflows to validate the audience, message and distribution channel first. Paid upgrades can be evaluated later only when a real limitation or return justifies them.

This guide is a starting toolkit—not a claim that all listed services are unlimited or that analytics are already installed on this portfolio website.
