Self-hosted vs hosted freelance management software

Self-hosting gives you control over deployment, but also places operational duties on you. Hosted services usually reduce infrastructure work while relying on a provider.

Self-hosted responsibilities

  • Installation and configuration.
  • Server and database maintenance.
  • Access control and security updates.
  • Backup planning and restore testing.
  • Monitoring, availability and troubleshooting.

Hosted service tradeoffs

  • Provider-managed infrastructure and updates, depending on service.
  • Subscription, service limits and provider dependency may apply.
  • Review privacy, export, availability and cancellation terms.
