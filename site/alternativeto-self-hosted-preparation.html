Name

Tagline

Self-hosted freelance business management for clients, projects, tasks, invoices and business tracking.

Description

The Freelance Command Center Self-Hosted is a freelance business management application designed to help independent service providers organize clients, projects, tasks and follow-ups, quotes and invoices, income and expenses, time tracking, goals and business analytics in one workspace. It runs on a customer-managed environment using Node.js and PostgreSQL. Customers are responsible for installation, hosting, access security, backups and maintenance.
