Editorial method

  • Each series follows chronology, not feature advertising.
  • Each article identifies the problem, observed evidence, constraints, options, decision, implementation, tests and remaining limitations.
  • Source records and repository artifacts take precedence over memory.
  • Customer-sensitive, security-sensitive and private information is redacted.
  • Claims about tests, deployments and releases require dated evidence.

This hub is a series roadmap and entry point, not proof that every episode is published or independently audited.
