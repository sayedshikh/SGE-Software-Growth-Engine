FAQ

Questions about SGE

What is Software Growth Engine?

SGE is a guided workspace designed to organize customer-problem discovery, evidence review, opportunity evaluation, response preparation and recorded campaign outcomes.

Does SGE automatically contact customers or post replies?

No. Drafts are prepared for human review. External publishing or messaging is performed by the user outside SGE.

Are opportunity scores guaranteed to be accurate?

No. Assessments use advisory heuristics and should be reviewed alongside source evidence, context and assumptions.

Does SGE verify sales or revenue?

No. Commercial outcomes are recorded from user-entered data and are not independently verified.

Can SGE guarantee customers, sales, traffic or rankings?

No. SGE does not guarantee sales, revenue, traffic, search rankings or AI-search placement.

Where does research come from?

Discovery and monitoring depend on supported sources and their access limitations. Users should review source relevance and respect source terms and community policies.

Is SGE complete and fully validated?

SGE is actively developed. Individual workflows may still require additional browser-level, live-source and commercial validation.

Where can I report feedback?

Use the public GitHub repository and its feedback issue template. Do not post secrets, sensitive personal information or customer data publicly.
