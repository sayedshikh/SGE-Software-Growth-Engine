Product overview

A connected workspace for customer-problem-led growth.

SGE organizes the steps from research signals to evidence review, opportunity assessment, contextual response drafts, conversation records and campaign outcomes.

Core workflow areas

  • Guided discovery: structure research around business, product, audience and goals.
  • Monitoring: review signals from supported sources, subject to source access and limitations.
  • Evidence: inspect source context and distinguish observations from assumptions.
  • Opportunity intelligence: use transparent heuristics to assess relevance and fit.
  • Match & Reply: prepare contextual drafts for human editing and external sending.
  • Conversation workflow: record stages and boundaries such as consent and refusal.
  • Campaign journey: link opportunities, artifacts, leads and user-entered outcomes.
  • Commercial tracking: aggregate entered costs and outcomes with currency-aware reporting.
  • Next-step guidance: suggest a step based on recorded workflow state.

Product maturity

SGE is actively evolving. This page describes documented scope; it is not a blanket claim that every workflow has completed browser-level, live-source or commercial validation.
