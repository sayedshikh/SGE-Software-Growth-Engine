Workflow & safeguards

Guidance with human control.

SGE supports the workflow while the user remains responsible for verifying evidence and deciding what to do externally.

How the journey works

  1. Configure business, product, audience and context.
  2. Discover or monitor supported signals.
  3. Review source evidence and limitations.
  4. Assess opportunity fit with advisory heuristics.
  5. Prepare, edit and manually publish or send replies outside SGE.
  6. Record conversations, consent, refusals and lead stages.
  7. Connect artifacts, campaigns and user-entered commercial outcomes.
  8. Review recorded metrics and suggested next steps.

Important boundaries

  • Source relevance and credibility require human judgment.
  • Opportunity scores are advisory, not guarantees.
  • SGE does not automatically post or send messages.
  • Recorded outcomes are not independently verified.
  • Financial aggregates rely on entered records and are kept distinct by currency.
  • No guaranteed sales, revenue, traffic, SEO rankings or AI-search placement.
