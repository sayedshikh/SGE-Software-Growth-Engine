Distribution playbook

Prepare a software listing before submitting

Use a repeatable preparation process for directories, launch platforms, marketplaces and community posts. Copy should be customized to each channel and based on verified product facts.

Submission checklist

  • Current product name, canonical product URL and support route.
  • One sentence positioning and concise description.
  • Audience, primary use case and clear problem.
  • Current pricing and license terms—verify from seller page before submission.
  • 2–5 authentic screenshots or short demo; never fabricated UI.
  • Logo, favicon and brand assets, if requested.
  • Privacy, data handling, support and platform requirements.
  • Honest alternatives and category selection.
  • Submission status, date, follow-up and outcome tracking.

Short copy template

Tagline: [Specific outcome] for [specific audience], without [relevant friction].

Short description: [Product] helps [audience] organize/complete [job] through [verified capabilities]. It is designed for [specific use case]. [One limitation or requirement].

CTA: Explore the product details or see the current seller listing.

Distribution tracking

Track each submission separately: channel, product, listing URL, submission URL, date, status, fee, approval, source traffic (if measured), and next action. A submission is not a result until verified.
