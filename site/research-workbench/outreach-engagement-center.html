Global Engagement & Conversation Center

A manual/local prototype for qualifying public problem signals, deciding whether engagement is appropriate, drafting helpful responses and managing the conversation lifecycle. It does not connect to social accounts, crawl platforms, send messages, post comments or run unattended.

Delegation, privacy and platform safety: this worksheet does not collect credentials, passwords, tokens or sensitive contact data. Account automation must use official APIs or approved platform tools, comply with each platform's rules, and be transparently delegated. Avoid deceptive identity claims, mass outreach, unsolicited DMs, repetitive comments, bypassing rate limits or manipulating discussions. Human approval is required before external posts/messages until an account-specific compliant integration and testing gate is established.

Workflow model

1 · Discovery

Record a public source, signal, problem, source date, URL and observed evidence.

2 · Qualification

Score product relevance, urgency, public engagement suitability and confidence; flag sensitive or ambiguous signals for non-engagement.

3 · Helpful response

Draft a context-aware answer with empathy, useful advice and no hard sell; cite only verified product features.

4 · Conversation

Record replies and context. Respect silence, refusal, opt-outs and requests to stop. Do not follow up indefinitely.

5 · Private transition

Invite a private discussion only where permitted, appropriate and clearly optional—not as a forced funnel.

6 · Product match

Offer a relevant product and truthful demo/listing only when the person's expressed need fits.

7 · Outcome learning

Track outcomes from real evidence, not impressions or fabricated conversion.

Signal & reply drafting workspace

Session registry

Records exist only in this browser session/page memory; refresh clears them. Export before leaving if needed. Do not enter private or sensitive information.

Automation maturity gates (not yet connected)

  1. Source catalogue and verified access methods per platform, locale and product.
  2. Compliant official API/connector review, account ownership and delegated permissions.
  3. Read-only ingestion and deduplication, with evidence provenance and rate-limit controls.
  4. Signal classification and risk/intent filters, including sensitive-topic exclusions.
  5. Drafting and policy-aware quality checks, with human review queue.
  6. Approved publishing/messaging integrations only where allowed; clear activity logs and stop controls.
  7. Conversation inbox, opt-out/refusal handling, bounded follow-up and escalation.
  8. Outcome analytics, safety audit, integration tests and staged rollout.

Prototype status: worksheet and draft generator only. No API integrations, monitoring, account delegation or live interaction have been tested or connected.
