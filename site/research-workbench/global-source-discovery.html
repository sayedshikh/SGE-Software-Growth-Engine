Global Source Discovery & Coverage Engine

A structured source inventory and research workflow for discovering public software-market problem signals across regions, languages, platforms and channel types. This prototype stores records in this browser session only. It does not crawl, subscribe, connect, scrape or contact sources.

Evidence boundary: source seeds are discovery starting points—not verified coverage or current availability. Verify official URLs, API terms, access restrictions, costs, locale coverage and signal quality before advancing any source to Verified. No credentials or personal data should be stored.

Coverage quality checklist

  • Verify each source's official domain and terms.
  • Record language and local market; do not assume English search results represent the market.
  • Prefer documented APIs, feeds or manual public research; do not bypass access controls.
  • Separate source verification from signal evidence and commercial intent.
  • Use freshness checks and deduplicate discussions across sources.
  • Keep sensitive, health, financial, crisis, minors, and other high-risk contexts out of marketing engagement.
  • Do not automate posting, messaging, or scraping where prohibited.
  • Document failure states, inaccessible sources, and coverage gaps.
