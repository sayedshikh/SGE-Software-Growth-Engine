Operating principles

  • External-first and product-relevant, not site-visitor-centric.
  • Verify sources; distinguish facts, observations and hypotheses.
  • Prioritize signals that indicate customers or credible customer-referral paths.
  • Respect platform rules, privacy, consent and reasonable boundaries.
  • Never automate unsolicited outreach or external posting by default.
  • Track evidence and real outcomes, never invented metrics.
