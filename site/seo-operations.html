Audit register

Advisory prioritization: impact × confidence ÷ effort. Severity remains a separate override.

Audit sequence

  1. Inventory and canonical URL scope.
  2. Status codes, redirects, crawlability, canonical, robots and sitemap alignment.
  3. Metadata, heading hierarchy, language, intent and content uniqueness.
  4. Internal links, orphan-page risk and cluster connectivity.
  5. Structured data validity and visible-content consistency.
  6. Accessibility, responsive usability and performance testing.
  7. Search Console/Bing diagnostics when access is available.
  8. Prioritize, implement, validate, document and re-audit.

Quality gates

  • Evidence recorded; distinguish live validation from assumption or checklist review.
  • Avoid doorway or near-duplicate pages.
  • Keep product entities, claims, offer data and visible content consistent.
  • Each change has a validation step; no ranking claims without measured evidence.
  • External changes and publication remain owner-reviewed.
