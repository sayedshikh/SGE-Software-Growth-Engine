Scoring & decision guidance

Advisory score = (Impact × Commercial relevance × Confidence) ÷ Effort. This prioritizes review, not guaranteed traffic or revenue. Evidence classification and risks should override score when necessary. Confidence should reflect source quality and corroboration—not optimism.

  • Observed: capture the source, date and contextual signal.
  • Corroborated: multiple independent signals support the same problem.
  • Hypothesis: plausible, but test before treating as demand evidence.
  • Map opportunities to existing pages before creating duplicates; use the Content Cluster mapper and Site Intelligence Link Graph.
  • Connect the next action to a distribution record, growth experiment or conversion journey.
