A reviewable workflow for subtitle localization

Subtitle localization is more than translating individual lines. Context, speaker intent, recurring terminology, reading flow and the subtitle file's structure all matter. This workflow helps organize the work and review it systematically.

1. Prepare the source

  • Confirm the subtitle format and language pair.
  • Keep an untouched copy of the source file.
  • Identify recurring character, product, place and technical names.
  • Gather context that is appropriate to share with the chosen translation provider.

2. Translate with context

  • Translate meaning and tone, not just word-for-word phrasing.
  • Maintain a glossary for recurring names and terms.
  • Review ambiguous jokes, idioms, cultural references and short cues that depend on surrounding dialogue.
  • Use only AI providers and data-sharing settings you have assessed for the material.

3. Protect subtitle structure

  • Check cue order and identifiers where the format uses them.
  • Inspect start and end timestamps.
  • Review line breaks and formatting tags.
  • Compare output against the original before accepting a batch.

4. Quality review

  • Check accuracy, tone and terminology consistency.
  • Read subtitles alongside the audio or video when possible.
  • Review readability, line length and timing.
  • Check names, numbers and negations carefully.
  • Do not assume AI output is error-free; retain a human review step.

Checklist before export

  • Terminology and names checked
  • Context-sensitive cues reviewed
  • Timing and cue order checked
  • Formatting and line breaks checked
  • Human review completed where needed
  • Output opened in a compatible subtitle player/editor

This is a general workflow guide. It is not a claim of certification, guaranteed translation quality or independently tested product performance.
