Product-to-channel fit matrix

Channel selection is product-specific. Priorities below are hypotheses based on product type and current status; they are not evidence of traffic, approval or sales.

Priority matrix

Freelance Command Center — Self-Hosted

Primary: SaaSHub and AlternativeTo, subject to owner submission and platform approval.

Secondary: self-hosted software communities, GitHub documentation/demo, Indie Hackers technical/product story, relevant freelancer communities where promotion is allowed.

Assets: install guide, security/backup responsibility disclosure, genuine screenshots, demo video, release/requirements checklist.

Gate: verify package, current price, support and screenshot claims.

Freelance Command Center — Android

Primary: product website and seller pages; Android-focused content and communities with posting rules; directories that accept independently distributed apps.

Secondary: SaaSHub candidate only if eligibility/site-quality conditions are met; AlternativeTo eligibility to be assessed separately.

Assets: genuine device screenshots, installation steps, compatibility statement, safe APK delivery guidance.

Gate: verify APK version, device compatibility, package safety, listing claims and refund/support terms.

Freelance Command Center — Excel

Primary: Payhip/Gumroad listings and useful spreadsheet-related content/tutorials.

Secondary: template marketplaces and creator/resource directories only after eligibility and fees are checked; community posts must be genuinely useful and permitted.

Assets: workbook previews, clear feature walkthrough, compatibility requirements, examples using synthetic data.

Gate: review workbook version, formulas, macro/privacy claims (if any), price and license.

CleanSubtitle AI

Primary: subtitle/video creator and localization communities, tutorials, relevant software directories, and its sales page—only after current release verification.

Secondary: AlternativeTo/SaaSHub if the package, product website and category eligibility are verified.

Assets: real before/after subtitle demo, supported formats/providers, timestamp preservation proof, system requirements and license details.

Gate: package/version and claims remain verification-needed.

Development products: SGE, SubAI Pro, RechnungPilot DE, CRM Enterprise, CRM Essential

Primary: development logs, technical documentation, demos, problem research and audience discovery—not finished-product directories yet.

Gate: do not submit as released software until readiness, actual product functionality, website, support and current package are verified.

Cross-channel scoring rubric

CriterionQuestionScore
Audience fitDo users on this channel actively seek this product category?0–5
IntentAre they researching or choosing a solution?0–5
EligibilityDoes product maturity and type meet current platform rules?0–5
CostCan we test it without spending, or with bounded cost?0–5
EvidenceCan we track visits/leads attributable to this channel?0–5
EffortIs the effort reasonable relative to expected learning?0–5
RiskDoes it avoid spam, privacy, platform-rule and reputational risks?0–5

Use the scores to compare opportunities, not to forecast revenue. Reassess if platform policies, product status or actual performance changes.

Experiment protocol

  1. Choose one product and one channel.
  2. Record baseline and hypothesis before posting/submitting.
  3. Prepare accurate assets and a tracking URL where permitted.
  4. Owner reviews and performs any external submission or posting.
  5. Record actual approval, referrals, visits, inquiries and outcomes only when evidenced.
  6. Decide whether to repeat, improve or stop based on cost, effort and evidence.
