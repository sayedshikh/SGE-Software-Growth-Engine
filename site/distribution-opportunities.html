Execution rules

  • One product per listing; do not bundle unrelated products into an unclear pitch.
  • Use genuine screenshots and current product descriptions; no fabricated reviews, user counts or capabilities.
  • Keep a record of URL, submission date, status, costs, response, referral traffic and conversions only when actual analytics are available.
  • Do not pay for listings, badges or advertising without explicit approval.
  • SGE remains in development. RechnungPilot DE, SubAI Pro and the CRM products are also in development; do not submit them as finished products until their status, demo, documentation, packaging and claims are verified. Use research and community participation to learn, not to imply availability.
