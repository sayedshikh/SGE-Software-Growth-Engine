There is no universal best tool. Choose based on your workflow, technical comfort, device preference and responsibility for data and maintenance.

Selection checklist

  • Do you primarily work on a phone, computer or both?
  • Are you comfortable editing spreadsheets?
  • Can you maintain your own server?
  • How will you back up and protect your data?
  • What does the seller's current license and support policy say?
