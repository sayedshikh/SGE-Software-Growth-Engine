Want to manage freelance operations on your own server?

Self-hosted software can offer deployment control, but it also means the operator takes responsibility for infrastructure, security, backups and maintenance.

When self-hosting may make sense

  • You want to run the application on infrastructure you manage.
  • You need a business workflow for clients, projects, tasks, invoices and expenses.
  • You can maintain Node.js and PostgreSQL requirements or have technical help.
  • You are willing to plan access controls, backups and updates.
