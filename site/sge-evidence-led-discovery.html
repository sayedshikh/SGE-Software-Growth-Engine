Product workflow

Evidence-led problem discovery

SGE's intended connected journey keeps problem signals, source review, fit evaluation and human-reviewed responses linked together.

Workflow model

  1. Discover potential customer problems.
  2. Monitor relevant signals.
  3. Review source evidence and provenance.
  4. Match problems to product context.
  5. Evaluate opportunity using advisory criteria.
  6. Prepare a reply for human review and track the conversation.
