Problem-solving guide · Subtitle editing

Subtitle text needs cleanup, but timing and structure must stay intact

Subtitle files can contain spelling, punctuation, transcription or formatting errors. Editing text while keeping cue timing and structure consistent requires careful review.

Common subtitle cleanup tasks

  • Correct obvious text errors and punctuation.
  • Review wording in context rather than cue by cue in isolation.
  • Protect timestamps and subtitle structure during edits.
  • Compare the original and cleaned text before accepting changes.
  • Keep a human review step for ambiguous content.
