Project profitability estimator

Estimate project profit, margin and effective hourly earnings. Calculations happen in this browser page; no submit or data upload is wired.

What the results mean

  • Estimated profit: fee minus direct costs and allocated overhead.
  • Profit margin: estimated profit divided by fee, when fee is above zero.
  • Effective hourly earnings: profit divided by recorded hours, not a salary or tax calculation.

Estimate only. This tool does not account for taxes, payment risk, opportunity cost or all business expenses unless you include them. Avoid entering confidential client information.
