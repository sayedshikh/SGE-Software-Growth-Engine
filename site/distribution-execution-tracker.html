AlternativeTo

Fit: software that is released and a credible alternative to a known application. Its official FAQ states unreleased, private beta, invite-only and early access products are generally not accepted; simple tools/calculators and certain content-like or clone products may also be declined.

Portfolio: Self-Hosted Freelance Command Center is the strongest initial candidate for careful review; Excel toolkit and Android edition should be assessed individually for category/duplicate concerns; CleanSubtitle AI only after verifying release. Cost: normal submission described as free; paid priority review optional and not approval. Status: preparation only.

Stage 2 — conditional opportunities

  • Show HN: only for a genuine technical demo, substantial story or tool that fits community expectations.
  • DevHunt / Peerlist Launchpad: verify fit, current rules and launch slot before preparing submission.
  • G2 / Capterra / GetApp: defer until product category eligibility and real customer-review base justify listing. No manufactured reviews.
  • Startup Stash / smaller directories: review one by one; skip paid listings, badge swaps and low-quality directories.

Readiness statuses

  • Draft: copy prepared.
  • Ready for owner review: claims, screenshots, package and destination link verified.
  • Submitted: owner has actually submitted externally.
  • Approved / live: verified public listing.
  • Rejected / paused: logged with reason and next action.
