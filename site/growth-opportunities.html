Opportunity intelligence

Growth intelligence & opportunity prioritization

A structured opportunity record separates an observed problem from a validated growth opportunity. Each score is a heuristic; evidence, fit, effort and risk remain visible.

Opportunity evaluation dimensions

  • Problem evidence: source, recency, context, corroboration and directness.
  • Audience fit: whether the signal concerns a clearly defined target audience.
  • Product fit: whether an existing verified product capability solves the problem or a credible development gap exists.
  • Channel fit: whether a channel reaches the audience with appropriate intent and rules.
  • Competitive gap: concrete observed difference, not generic competitor assertions.
  • Commercial potential: qualitative assessment; not a revenue forecast.
  • Effort: estimated resource demand.
  • Risk: legal, privacy, platform, reputational, financial, technical and claim risk.

Score model

Each factor except effort and risk is rated 1–5. Evidence strength, audience fit, product fit, channel fit and competitive gap are scored separately. Score is advisory:

Opportunity score = (Evidence × Audience × Product × Channel × Gap × Commercial) ÷ Effort.

Review risk separately and never automatically select high-risk opportunities.

Decision rules

  • Accepted: evidence and fit justify a specific next action; acceptance is not a forecast.
  • Needs evidence: problem signal is weak, stale, indirect or insufficiently corroborated.
  • Deferred: potentially relevant, but dependencies, timing or resources make it lower priority.
  • Rejected: poor product fit, invalid audience, high risk or unsupported premise.
  • Converted to experiment: linked to a defined experiment, metric and success criteria.
