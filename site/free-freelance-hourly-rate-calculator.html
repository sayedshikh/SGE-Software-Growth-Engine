Freelance hourly rate calculator

Estimate a planning rate based on your income goal, business expenses, reserve and realistic billable hours. This is a simple estimate, not financial or tax advice.

Enter values and calculate.

How this estimate works

It adds your target income and expenses, then applies the reserve percentage as a gross-up factor and divides by billable hours. The reserve is an assumption, not a tax rate recommendation.

Formula: (income target + expenses) ÷ (1 − reserve fraction) ÷ billable hours.
