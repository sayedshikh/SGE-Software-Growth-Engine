Excel vs a freelance management app

A spreadsheet and a dedicated app solve different organizational needs. The right choice depends on how you work.

Spreadsheet strengths

  • Familiar grid-based editing.
  • Flexible calculations and layout.
  • Portable file workflow.

App strengths

  • Interface designed around mobile workflows.
  • Purpose-built forms and navigation.
  • Potentially easier routine entry for users who prefer app interfaces.

Tradeoffs

Spreadsheets require organization and care with formulas and file backups. Apps require compatibility checks, understanding storage and backup behavior, and reviewing licensing and delivery details.
