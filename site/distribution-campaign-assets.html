Campaign preparation system

Distribution campaign asset system

Reusable, product-specific asset standards to reduce repeated preparation and protect against outdated or unsupported claims. This is a template system, not evidence that every asset has been created or verified.

Required product campaign bundle

  1. Product truth sheet: verified product name, current version, platform, requirements, confirmed features, limitations, license, price/date checked, support/refund routes.
  2. Message set: one-line description, tagline, short description, long description, audience/problem framing, CTA, FAQs, objection responses.
  3. Media: genuine screenshots, logo/icon, video/demo, captions, image alt text; each tied to a package/version and capture date.
  4. Channel-specific copy: categories, tags, competitors, URL, submission notes, channel rules, character limits and required fields.
  5. Tracking: campaign ID, source/medium/campaign UTM, landing page, date, approval status, outbound clicks and recorded outcomes when available.
  6. Review gate: owner approval, evidence checks, platform rules, privacy and licensing review.

Version and claim control

  • Every bundle has a product/version/date checked record.
  • Unsupported or unverified claims are flagged and excluded.
  • Prices and marketplace links must be rechecked before use.
  • Use demo/synthetic data in screenshots unless real data is explicitly permitted and privacy-safe.
  • Do not claim rankings, testimonials, sales, downloads, customers or performance without evidence.
  • Maintain draft, owner-reviewed, submitted, live and retired states.

UTM convention

utm_source=[platform]
utm_medium=directory|community|content|launch
utm_campaign=[product]-[release]-[campaign]
utm_content=[asset-or-copy-variant]

Keep values lowercase, concise and stable. Avoid personal information. Confirm that tracking is lawful, privacy-safe and accepted by the destination platform.
