# Software Growth Engine

SGE is designed to help a software seller or small business move from customer-problem discovery to evidence review, opportunity evaluation, response preparation, conversation tracking and recorded commercial outcomes in one workspace.

## Journey

1. Configure business, product and audience context.
2. Discover or monitor public problem signals using supported sources.
3. Review source evidence and limitations.
4. Evaluate product/problem fit using transparent heuristics.
5. Prepare reply drafts; edit and publish/send manually outside SGE.
6. Record conversations and lead stages, respecting consent and opt-out.
7. Link campaign artifacts, leads, outcomes and recorded costs.
8. Review financial metrics and suggested next steps based on entered records.

SGE does not independently verify sales or revenue and does not automate unsolicited outreach.
